About HB Aeromotive

Harold Bickford has been flying model aircraft for decades — from high-wing trainers to scale warbirds. HB Aeromotive is how he shares that passion: quality used and new equipment at honest prices, from someone who actually flies.

Located in Nebraska. Sales are primarily local pickup, with shipping available for smaller items. Every item is inspected and described accurately.

If you're just getting started, Harold is happy to point you toward the right trainer and help you get in the air.
